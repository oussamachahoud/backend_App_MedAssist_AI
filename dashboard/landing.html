💡 About The Platform

What is MedAssist AI?

The first Algerian academic project leveraging Multimodal Deep Learning with Gated Cross-Attention to bridge the gap between AI research and practical telemedicine.

📱

Smartphone-Based Telemedicine

Eliminates reliance on costly dermatoscopes. Clinicians and patients can capture clinical photographs directly using standard smartphone cameras under realistic conditions.

🧠

Multimodal Gated Fusion

Unlike image-only models, MedAssist AI correlates visual features with 7 standardized clinical variables (age, sex, location, diameter, bleeding, growth, history) via Gated Cross-Attention.

📋

Clinical Triage & PDF Reports

Implements a 4-tier clinical risk escalation protocol (LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, CRITICAL) and instantly generates exportable PDF diagnostic reports for electronic medical records.

⚙️ Clinical Workflow

Simple 4-Step Diagnosis

An intuitive guided workflow designed for rapid consultation in under 2 minutes.

1
📸

Guided Capture

Capture or upload the skin lesion using our circular centering overlay with real-time illumination assistance.

2
📝

Clinical Metadata

Fill 7 structured parameters: age, sex, lesion location, diameter, bleeding, recent growth, and cancer history.

3
🤖

Multimodal Fusion

EfficientNet-B3 extracts 49 visual patches while the MLP branch processes metadata via Gated Cross-Attention.

4
📊

Triage & PDF Report

Review class probabilities, color-coded clinical risk level, actionable next steps, and download the full PDF.

✨ Platform Capabilities

Engineered for Healthcare Rigor

Combining advanced deep learning, medical safety safeguards, and modern software engineering.

🔬

6 Skin Lesion Pathologies

Classifies Actinic Keratosis (ACK), Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC), Melanoma (MEL), Nevus (NEV), Squamous Cell Carcinoma (SCC), and Seborrheic Keratosis (SEK).

🎯

Optimal Calibrated Thresholds

Replaces naive argmax with pathology-specific optimal thresholds (θc) to recalibrate precision-recall trade-offs for lethal conditions like Melanoma.

🛡️

Automated Safety Escalation

If prediction confidence falls below 50%, the safety engine automatically bumps the clinical risk level by one tier and mandates manual dermatologist review.

📚

ABCDE Educational Module

Interactive clinical guide teaching physicians and patients the international ABCDE rule (Asymmetry, Border, Color, Diameter, Evolution) for early skin cancer detection.

📄

Exportable PDF Medical Dossier

Generates complete, standardized PDF diagnostic summaries with full probability bars, risk badges, patient metadata, and medical disclaimer.

🔒

Privacy & Zero Data Leakage

Strict adherence to GDPR data minimization: images and clinical data are processed strictly in volatile memory (RAM) and never permanently stored on servers.
